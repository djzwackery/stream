Twitch relay

Subscribes directly to Twitch's EventSub for Channel Point redemptions and forwards them into every redemptions.html/ alerts.html source on this origin, the same way control.html does. This is separate from the Streamlabs relay because Streamlabs doesn't relay redemptions at all.

  1. Register an app at dev.twitch.tv/console/apps. Any name, category "Application Integration" is fine.
  2. Add this exact URL to its OAuth Redirect URLs: . Add both your local http://localhost:5500/twitch-relay.html and the deployed URL if you use both.
  3. Copy the app's Client ID (not the Client Secret, this page never needs it) into the field below.
